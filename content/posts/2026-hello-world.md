The goal for this site is simple: publish without friction, keep the interface memorable, and avoid turning the personal website into a heavy application.

Content is edited through a Git-backed CMS and compiled before deployment. The reader gets static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The author gets structured content without a database to maintain.
