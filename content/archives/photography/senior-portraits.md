Senior portrait photography sessions for RISE Academy students.

RISE Academy Senior Portraits is a portrait photography collection from Awaiten Films, dated June 2024. The collection includes 6 photographs in the original collection order, created for RISE Academy.

Working closely with the school, Awaiten provided an opportunity for students to receive high quality portraits with multiple background and clothing options.
