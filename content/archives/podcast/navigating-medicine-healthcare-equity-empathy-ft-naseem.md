Join Muhammad Hadi Yusufali and Ali Almathkur as they speak with Naseem Rangwala, an internal medicine resident at UCSF. Throughout the episode, she shares her unique journey from public health at Berkeley to medical school at UCLA, and now to her residency. Naseem discusses the challenges of merging her medical career with her commitment to public health and community outreach, the impact of COVID-19 on her education, and how she navigated personal and professional obstacles.
