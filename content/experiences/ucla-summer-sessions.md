UCLA helped sharpen the way I think about story before a camera ever turns on. The strongest lessons were practical: how scenes carry pressure, how performances change a cut, and how structure can make a small piece feel bigger than its budget.

What stayed with me:

- A more disciplined way to break down scripts, scenes, and character motivation.
- Classroom critique from instructors with real production experience.
- A stronger foundation for the directing, editing, and documentary work I took on afterward.
