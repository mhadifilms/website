Highlights:

- Co-founded the studio with Aalyan Aamir to make high-quality multimedia production more accessible.
- Worked across directing, cinematography, editing, motion graphics, and client delivery.
- Helped produce community, brand, documentary, and event work that collectively reached 1M+ views.
