Journey Tellers started as a way to have slower, better conversations than social media usually allows. I co-hosted with Ali Almathkur and worked across guest research, interview prep, production, editing, and distribution.

Highlights:

- Hosted founders, community builders, creatives, and people with stories that deserved more time.
- Produced episodes for YouTube and major podcast platforms.
- Used the show as a way to practice listening, pacing, and long-form narrative.
