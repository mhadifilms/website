Journey Tellers was built around a simple premise: real people, real stories, real insights. I co-hosted with Ali Almathkur and helped shape the show across research, guest conversations, production, editing, and distribution.

Highlights:

- Hosted conversations with founders, community builders, creatives, and people carrying under-told stories.
- Produced episodes for YouTube and major podcast platforms, balancing narrative depth with accessible long-form media.
- Built the project as both a creative outlet and a way to document the story of the Muslim West from the inside.
