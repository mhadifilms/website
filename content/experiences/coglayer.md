coglayer was a product and growth chapter: part positioning, part product management, and part figuring out what students and users actually needed from a tool that called itself training wheels for your brain.

Highlights:

- Helped build and scale from 0 to 35,000 users.
- Worked across product management, project management, beta testing, marketing, and SEO.
- Redesigned the initial UI prototype around floating cards and interactive questions so the interface could adapt to the user instead of feeling like a static lesson page.
- Helped translate the product idea into clearer messaging, feedback loops, and launch motion.
